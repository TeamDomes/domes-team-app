# Domes Team App — For Budtenders

Welcome to the Domes Team App! This is your hub for games, recognition, and rewards.

**What's inside:**

- **Trivia** — Daily cannabis knowledge quiz. Get smarter, earn points.
- **Wordle** — Cannabis-themed word puzzle. One word per day.
- **Boggle** — Challenge a coworker to a cannabis word hunt. Study the word list first!
- **BINGO** — Weekly card with goals like being on time, upselling, and getting Google reviews. Complete a row to win 1,500 points.
- **Grower of the Week** — Learn about a featured brand and take the quiz.
- **Appreciations** — Shout out a teammate who helped you or made your day better.
- **Wall of Love** — See what customers are saying about us on Google.
- **My Stats** — Track your sales performance week over week.
- **Pets & Spotted** — Share your pets and cool finds with the team.

**Points & Rewards:**

- Earn points from games, quizzes, BINGO, and Google Review mentions.
- When a customer mentions you by name in a 4-5 star Google Review, you earn **250 points**.
- Game points are capped at 500/week — but Google mentions and BINGO wins have no cap.
- **500 pts = $5** | **1,000 pts = $15** | **1,500 pts = $25 or Secret Stash Grab**

Go to **domes-team-app-mu.vercel.app** and log in with your email.

# Domes Team App — For Team Leads

Welcome to the Domes Team App! Everything above applies to you, plus some extras.

**Lead-specific features:**

- **Points Report** — View everyone's weekly and all-time points. Export CSV for AIQ loyalty updates.
- **Team Stats** — See performance metrics across the whole team.
- **Import Stats** — Upload weekly Dutchie reports to update budtender stats.
- **Import Catalog** — Import product catalogs for the Reviews feature.
- **Trivia Admin** — Add new trivia questions.

**How Lead points work:**

You automatically earn **1/3 of every point your budtenders earn**. When your team is active and engaged, your points grow too. There's no weekly cap on lead points — your rewards reflect how well you're building the team.

**Admin tools** are accessible from the links at the top of your dashboard.

Go to **domes-team-app-mu.vercel.app** and log in with your email.
